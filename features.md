# Company Management Dashboard Features

Based on your current application architecture (Invoices, Tasks, LMS, Meetings, etc.), here is a comprehensive list of features for a centralized company management dashboard. These are divided into currently supported areas (which can be enhanced) and suggested new features.

## 1. Main Overview Dashboard (The "Command Center")
The single pane of glass you see when you first log in.
- **KPI Widgets:** Quick stats on Total Revenue (Current Month/Year), Outstanding Invoices, Open Tasks, and Upcoming Meetings.
- **Recent Activity Feed:** A timeline of recent actions across the company (e.g., "Invoice #102 created," "Task 'X' completed by John").
- **Quick Actions:** Buttons to instantly "Create Invoice," "Schedule Meeting," or "Add Task."
- **Notifications & Alerts:** Pending approvals, overdue invoices, or tasks past their deadline.

## 2. Financial Management (Invoicing & Billing)
- **Invoice Generation & PDF Export:** Create tax-compliant invoices (GST/VAT) with custom branding.
- **Payment Tracking:** Mark invoices as paid/pending and track partial payments.
- **Recurring Invoices:** Automated generation for retainer clients.
- **Expense Tracking:** Log company expenses and map them against revenues to calculate profit margins.
- **Financial Reports:** Visual charts (bar graphs, line charts) showing revenue growth, month-over-month comparisons.

## 3. Project & Task Management
- **Kanban Board View:** Drag-and-drop interface for tasks (To Do, In Progress, Review, Done).
- **Time Tracking:** Log billable/non-billable hours against specific tasks or projects.
- **Task Assignment & Deadlines:** Assign tasks to specific employees with priorities (Low, Medium, High).
- **Project Milestones:** Group tasks into larger projects and track overall completion percentage.
- **File Attachments:** Upload project-related documents directly to tasks.

## 4. CRM & Client Management
- **Client Database:** A directory of all client profiles, contact persons, and communication history.
- **Lead Tracking:** Track potential clients from initial contact to conversion (Sales Pipeline).
- **Client Portal (Optional):** A secure area where clients can log in to view their invoices and project progress.

## 5. Learning Management System (LMS)
*Since your company handles training/courses:*
- **Course & Batch Management:** Track active training batches, schedules, and enrolled students.
- **Curriculum Builder:** Manage training modules, lessons, and assignments.
- **Student Progress Tracking:** Monitor attendance, assignment completion, and grades.
- **Certification:** Automatically generate completion certificates for students.

## 6. Meetings & Scheduling
- **Calendar Integration:** Sync with Google Calendar or Outlook.
- **Meeting Logs & Minutes:** Store agendas, meeting links (Zoom/Google Meet), and post-meeting notes.
- **Follow-up Reminders:** Automated nudges to follow up with clients after a meeting.

## 7. HR & Employee Management
- **Employee Directory & Roles:** Manage user accounts, permissions, and profiles. Standard roles include: Administrator, Project Manager, Employee, Finance, Instructor, and Student.
- **Leave & Attendance Management:** Employees can request time off; managers can approve.
- **Performance Reviews:** Track employee KPIs, completed tasks, and generated revenue.

## 8. Reporting & Analytics
- **Custom Report Builder:** Generate PDF or CSV reports for any data point (e.g., "All overdue invoices for Q1").
- **Resource Allocation:** See who is overworked and who has spare capacity.
- **System Logs & Auditing:** Track which user deleted or modified critical records for security purposes.

---

### Implementation Phases
If you want to build this out, I recommend tackling it in phases:
1. **Phase 1 (Core):** Master the KPI Dashboard, Invoices, and Task Management.
2. **Phase 2 (Growth):** Add Meetings, CRM/Client Profiles, and LMS features.
3. **Phase 3 (Enterprise):** Add Expense tracking, HR modules, and advanced Analytics.
